Soul Print — Understand Yourself, Choose Your Next Step

Inspired by Alex’s search for understanding across psychology, philosophy, personality frameworks, and spiritual traditions. Use different perspectives thoughtfully, without reducing yourself to a label.

Know what each lens can tell you

Big Five research provides an established trait framework; this app’s particular questions and scoring are not thereby clinically validated. MindSet Matrix 16 and Inner Operating System / IOS-9 are original reflection concepts inspired by personality-typing and motivation frameworks. Jungian archetypes, narrative prompts, and shadow-work exercises offer interpretive reflection, not diagnosis.

Astrology, numerology, Human Design, and other spiritual systems are optional symbolic lenses. They are not scientific personality measurements. Philosophy asks about values, meaning, and how to live; it does not produce a clinical score.

A short values exercise

Choose three values: creativity, honesty, care, independence, stability, learning, fairness, belonging. Describe one recent choice that supported a value and one that conflicted with it. What is one small action you can take this week?

Communication & relationships

Complete: “When ____ happens, I feel ____. The story I tell myself is ____. Another possible explanation is ____. What I need to ask clearly is ____.” Describe behavior rather than assigning motives. Reflect on your own choices without using a test result to define another person.

Optional symbolic reflection

If a spiritual symbol or astrological theme feels meaningful, write what it brings to mind and compare it with your lived experience. You can skip this completely. No birth-chart placements are calculated on this worksheet, and personality or mental health cannot be established from facial appearance.

Your data and your choice

This worksheet sends no responses or images to an external service. Notes are saved only when you choose Save on this device. Export before clearing browser storage if you want to keep them. Use Delete saved notes to remove this worksheet’s stored responses; exported copies remain under your control. Avoid saving sensitive information on shared devices.

Your working pages

Fill these in, export a copy, or print blank pages. Responses stay in this browser; this page does not send them to a server.

Shared devices: export or print what you need, then delete saved notes. Printing opens your browser’s print dialog.
